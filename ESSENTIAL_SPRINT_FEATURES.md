# Essential Sprint Features - Must Have

## 🎯 Critical Features (Must Implement)

### 1. **Sprint Backlog View** ⭐⭐⭐
**Why Essential:**
- Teams need to see unassigned tasks during sprint planning
- Without this, users can't easily add tasks to sprints
- Core to the sprint planning workflow

**What it needs:**
- View of all tasks NOT assigned to any sprint
- Ability to assign tasks to sprint (drag-and-drop or bulk select)
- Filter/search capabilities
- Show task details (priority, assignee, due date)

**Implementation Priority:** HIGHEST

---

### 2. **Sprint Burndown Chart** ⭐⭐⭐
**Why Essential:**
- Primary metric for tracking sprint progress
- Shows if team is on track to complete sprint
- Industry standard for Agile/Scrum teams
- Helps identify blockers early

**What it needs:**
- Chart showing remaining work (task count or story points) over time
- Ideal burndown line (expected progress)
- Actual burndown line (actual progress)
- Daily updates

**Implementation Priority:** HIGHEST

---

### 3. **Sprint Start/Complete Actions** ⭐⭐
**Why Essential:**
- Gives teams explicit control over sprint lifecycle
- Better than auto-detection alone
- Allows teams to start/complete sprints when ready (not just by date)
- Can trigger notifications and reports

**What it needs:**
- "Start Sprint" button (changes status from Planning → Active)
- "Complete Sprint" button (changes status from Active → Completed)
- Confirmation dialogs
- Optional: Sprint completion summary/report

**Implementation Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. **Sprint Goal Progress Tracking** ⭐⭐
**Why Essential:**
- Sprint goal is already in the model, but no way to track progress
- Teams need to know if they're meeting their sprint goal
- Visual indicator helps keep team focused

**What it needs:**
- Visual indicator showing progress toward sprint goal
- Link sprint goal to specific tasks (optional but helpful)
- Show goal completion percentage

**Implementation Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH

---

## 🔶 Nice to Have (But Not Essential)

### Medium Priority
- Sprint Retrospective (important for improvement, but not blocking)
- Sprint Velocity Tracking (requires story points - can add later)
- Sprint Capacity Planning (helpful but not critical)
- Sprint Templates/Cloning (time-saver, not essential)

### Low Priority
- Sprint Comments/Discussions
- Sprint Attachments
- Sprint Dependencies
- Sprint Custom Fields
- Sprint Export
- Sprint Notifications
